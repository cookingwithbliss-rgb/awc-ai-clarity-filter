The AI Clarity Filter

Before you act on any AI-generated idea, plan, or suggestion. Run it through these 5 questions. Takes 3 minutes. Tells you exactly whether it deserves your focus this week.

Think of one AI idea you are considering acting on right now
Step 1: Name the idea

What is the AI idea or suggestion you are considering?

Write it in one sentence. The more specific, the sharper your result will be.

Step 2: Run the filter

Answer yes or no for each question.

Answer honestly, not hopefully. If you answer no, a follow-up will appear to help you turn it into a commitment.

01

Does this move the one thing that is most stuck in your business right now?

Not something interesting. Not something you could do. The specific thing that, if it moved, would change how this week feels.

Good. It is pointed at the right target.

If it is not moving the stuck thing, it is a distraction this week, even if it is a good idea.

What is the one thing that is actually stuck right now?

02

Can you finish this in the time you actually have this week?

Not ideal time. Not if everything goes well. The realistic hours available between now and the end of the week.

It fits. Keep going.

An idea you cannot finish this week is a distraction this week. It belongs in a future list.

What is the smallest version of this you could actually finish this week?

03

Will you know by Friday whether it worked?

There should be a clear signal, a number, a response, a visible result, that tells you if this was the right move. No signal means no plan. Just a hope.

It is testable. That is what makes it worth doing.

Name the signal before you start so you are not guessing at the end of the week.

What will you watch to know if this is moving in the right direction?

04

Is this your idea, or just a good-sounding AI suggestion?

AI knows patterns. It does not know your business, your energy, or where you actually are right now. A suggestion that sounds right is not the same as one that is right for you.

You own it. That matters more than it sounds.

Ask yourself what you would do if AI had not suggested this. That answer tells you everything.

What would you focus on this week if AI had not suggested this?

05

If you do this and nothing else this week, will you feel like the week moved something forward?

Strip everything else away. If this one thing gets done and nothing else does. Is that a week worth having?

That is the answer. This deserves your focus this week.

Then this is not the right thing. The week should feel like it moved something, not just like it was busy.

What would make this week feel like it actually moved something forward?
